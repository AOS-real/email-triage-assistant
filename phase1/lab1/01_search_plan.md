# Search plan

Problem sentence: People who want to check and question why an AI email-triage assistant made a recommendation cannot do so, because the system sorts their email but hides the signals behind each decision.

## Concept blocks (two or three terms each; take synonyms from the first abstracts you read)

| Block |     What it covers     |                  Terms                   |
|-------|------------------------|------------------------------------------|
|   A   | the person's behaviour | "over-reliance", overreliance, reliance  |
|   B   | the system's property  | explanation, rationale, transparency     |
|   C   | the setting            | "AI-assisted decision", assistant, agent |

## One natural-language question (for tools that read questions)

Does showing an AI system's explanations or reasoning reduce over-reliance on its recommendations?

## One Boolean query (for tools that match strings: OR inside a block, AND between blocks)

("over-reliance" OR overreliance OR reliance) AND (explanation OR rationale OR transparency) AND ("AI-assisted decision" OR assistant OR agent)

A block with no synonyms is probably your project's name, not a concept.
